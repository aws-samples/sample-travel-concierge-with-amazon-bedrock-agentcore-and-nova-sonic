Special Assistance Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
1. Wheelchair and Mobility Assistance
1.1 Requesting Assistance
  · Request wheelchair assistance at least 48 hours before departure for guaranteed
availability.
  · Same-day requests are accommodated on a best-effort basis.
  · Request via: online booking, manage trip, phone, or at the airport.
1.2 Types of Assistance
WCHR: Wheelchair to/from aircraft door (can walk short distances and climb stairs).
WCHS: Wheelchair to/from seat (cannot climb stairs but can walk to seat). WCHC:
Wheelchair to/from seat (completely immobile, must be carried).
1.3 Mobility Devices
  · Manual wheelchairs: Transported free of charge as checked baggage (does not count
toward bag allowance).
  · Power wheelchairs and scooters: Transported free of charge. Must have battery
information available.
  · Dry cell, gel cell, lithium-ion batteries: Accepted. Battery may remain
installed.
  · Spillable (wet cell) batteries: Must be disconnected and terminals insulated.
  · Walkers, canes, crutches: Carried on board at no charge.
1.4 No Charge
All wheelchair and mobility assistance services are provided at no additional cost.
2. Service Animals
2.1 Accepted Service Animals
In accordance with U.S. Department of Transportation regulations, only trained
service dogs are accepted in the aircraft cabin at no charge. The dog must be trained
to perform a task or work for a person with a disability, harnessed, leashed, or
tethered at all times, and able to fit within the handler's foot space or on the
handler's lap (small dogs).
2.2 Documentation Required
  · DOT Service Animal Transportation Form (completed by the passenger).
  · DOT Service Animal Relief Attestation Form (for flights over 8 hours).
  · Forms must be submitted at least 48 hours before departure.
2.3 Emotional Support Animals
Emotional support animals are not accepted as service animals. They may travel as
pets under the pet travel policy (cabin or cargo, with applicable fees).
3. Medical Equipment and Oxygen
3.1 Portable Oxygen Concentrators (POC)
  · FAA-approved POCs are permitted in the cabin at no charge.
  · Must have sufficient battery life for the flight duration plus 150%.
  · Notify us at least 48 hours before departure.
3.2 Other Medical Equipment
  · CPAP machines: Permitted as carry-on (does not count toward carry-on allowance).
  · Insulin and syringes: Permitted in carry-on with documentation.
  · Medications: Carry all medications in your carry-on bag. No quantity limits for
prescription medications.
  · Medical devices (pacemakers, insulin pumps): No restrictions. Inform TSA at
security.
3.3 Stretcher Service
For passengers who cannot sit upright, stretcher service is available on select
aircraft. Contact our medical assistance desk at least 72 hours before departure.
Additional fees apply.
4. Unaccompanied Minors
4.1 Age Requirements
Under 5: Must travel with an adult (18+). Ages 5-7: Unaccompanied minor service
required on direct flights only. Ages 8-14: Unaccompanied minor service required on
all flights. Ages 15-17: Unaccompanied minor service optional.
4.2 Service Details
  · Fee: $150 per child, per direction (one-way).
  · Includes: Escort through the airport, supervision during the flight, handoff to
the designated adult at arrival.
  · The child must have a valid ID and the booking confirmation.
  · A responsible adult (18+) must drop off and pick up the child. Photo ID required.
  · Not available on connecting flights for children under 8.
  · Not available on the last flight of the day.
5. Passengers with Allergies
5.1 Nut Allergies
  · We do not serve peanuts on any flight.
  · Tree nut products may be served. We cannot guarantee a nut-free environment.
  · If you have a severe nut allergy, notify us at least 48 hours before departure.
  · We will create a buffer zone (3 rows in each direction) where no nut products are
served.
  · We recommend carrying your own epinephrine auto-injector (EpiPen).
5.2 Other Allergies
  · Inform the cabin crew of any food allergies before meal service.
  · Special meals can be pre-ordered for most allergies (gluten-free, dairy-free,
etc.).
  · We cannot guarantee that any meal is completely free of specific allergens due to
shared preparation facilities.
6. Hearing and Vision Impairments
  · Safety briefing cards available in Braille on request.
  · Individual safety briefings provided by cabin crew.
  · Seat assignment near cabin crew for assistance during emergencies.
  · Captioned in-flight entertainment available on most aircraft.
  · Service animals for hearing or vision impairment accepted per Section 2.
7. How to Request Special Assistance
  · At booking: Select special assistance options during the booking process.
  · After booking: Log in to manage trip and add assistance requests.
  · Phone: Call our special assistance desk (available 24/7).
  · At the airport: Inform the check-in agent or gate agent.
  · Advance notice: 48 hours recommended for most services; 72 hours for stretcher
service.
