Pet Travel Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
1. Cabin Pets
1.1 Accepted Animals
Only cats and dogs are accepted as cabin pets. All other animals must travel as
cargo.
1.2 Requirements
  · The pet must be at least 8 weeks old.
  · The pet must remain in an airline-approved carrier for the entire flight.
  · The carrier must fit under the seat in front of you.
  · Maximum carrier dimensions: 19 x 13 x 9 inches (48 x 33 x 23 cm).
  · Combined weight of pet and carrier must not exceed 20 lbs (9 kg).
  · The pet must be odor-free and not cause a disturbance.
1.3 Fees
Domestic (U.S., Puerto Rico, USVI): $125 each way. U.S. to/from Canada: $125. U.S.
to/from Mexico: $125. U.S. to/from Caribbean/Central America: $125. U.S. to/from
South America: $200. U.S. to/from Europe: $200.
1.4 Limits
  · Maximum 1 pet carrier per passenger.
  · Maximum 7 cabin pets per flight (first come, first served).
  · Reserve your pet's spot at least 48 hours before departure by calling
reservations.
1.5 Carrier Requirements
  · Must be leak-proof and well-ventilated.
  · Soft-sided carriers recommended for under-seat fit.
  · Hard-sided carriers accepted if they meet size requirements.
  · The pet must be able to stand, turn around, and lie down comfortably.
2. Cargo Pets
2.1 When Cargo Is Required
  · Pets that exceed cabin size/weight limits.
  · Breeds not accepted in the cabin.
  · Routes where cabin pets are not permitted.
2.2 Cargo Pet Service
Cargo pet transport is handled through our cargo division. Contact our cargo team at
least 5 business days before travel.
2.3 Cargo Fees
Under 50 lbs (with crate): $250 domestic, $400 international. 50-100 lbs (with
crate): $350 domestic, $550 international. Over 100 lbs (with crate): $500 domestic,
$750 international.
2.4 Temperature Restrictions
For the safety of your pet, cargo transport is not available when:
  · Ground temperature at origin, destination, or any connection exceeds 85F (29C).
  · Ground temperature at origin, destination, or any connection is below 45F (7C).
  · Short-nosed (brachycephalic) breeds: Temperature must be below 75F (24C).
3. Breed Restrictions
3.1 Brachycephalic (Short-Nosed) Breeds - Cargo Restrictions
The following breeds are NOT accepted for cargo travel due to higher risk of
respiratory distress:
Dogs: American Bulldog, American Pit Bull Terrier, American Staffordshire Terrier,
Boston Terrier, Boxer, Brussels Griffon, Bulldog (all types), Cane Corso, Cavalier
King Charles Spaniel, Chow Chow, Dogue de Bordeaux, English Toy Spaniel, Japanese
Chin, Lhasa Apso, Mastiff (all types), Pekingese, Pug, Shar-Pei, Shih Tzu,
Staffordshire Bull Terrier, Tibetan Spaniel.
Cats: Burmese, Exotic Shorthair, Himalayan, Persian, Scottish Fold.
These breeds may travel in the cabin if they meet cabin pet requirements.
3.2 Banned Breeds
  · Wolf hybrids
  · Any animal that appears aggressive or dangerous
4. International Pet Travel
4.1 Documentation Required
  · Health certificate issued by a licensed veterinarian within 10 days of travel.
  · Proof of current rabies vaccination (at least 30 days before travel).
  · Destination country import permit (if required).
  · Microchip identification (ISO 11784/11785 compliant).
4.2 Country-Specific Requirements
Requirements vary significantly by destination. It is the passenger's responsibility
to research and comply with all destination country requirements. Some countries
require blood titer tests (up to 6 months before travel), quarantine periods upon
arrival, and specific parasite treatments within set timeframes.
5. Service Animals vs. Pets
Service animals (trained service dogs) travel free of charge in the cabin under our
Special Assistance Policy. They are not subject to pet fees, carrier requirements, or
cabin pet limits.
6. How to Book Pet Travel
  · Cabin pets: Call reservations at least 48 hours before departure. Cannot be
booked online.
  · Cargo pets: Contact our cargo division at least 5 business days before travel.
  · Payment: Pet fees are collected at the airport check-in counter.
  · Check-in: Arrive at least 2 hours early (domestic) or 3 hours early
(international) when traveling with a pet.
