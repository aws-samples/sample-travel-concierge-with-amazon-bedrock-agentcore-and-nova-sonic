Meal and Dining Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
1. Available Meal Options
1.1 Standard Meal Types
All passengers may choose from the following meal options at no additional charge:
  · Regular: The standard meal of the day. No dietary restrictions.
  · Vegetarian: No meat, poultry, or fish. May include dairy and eggs.
  · Vegan: No animal products of any kind, including dairy, eggs, and honey.
  · Kosher: Prepared in accordance with Jewish dietary law. Sealed and certified.
  · Halal: Prepared in accordance with Islamic dietary law.
  · Gluten-Free: No wheat, barley, rye, or gluten-containing ingredients.
1.2 Default Meal
If no preference is selected, the Regular meal is served by default on flights where
meals are provided.
2. Pre-Ordering a Special Meal
2.1 How to Pre-Order
Special meals (any option other than Regular) must be requested in advance:
  · Online: Log in and select Manage Trip, then choose your meal preference.
  · Phone: Call reservations and request your meal preference.
  · Deadline: Special meals must be requested at least 24 hours before departure.
    Requests made inside 24 hours cannot be guaranteed.
2.2 Changing Your Meal Preference
You may change your meal preference any time up to 24 hours before departure through
Manage Trip or by phone. Changes requested after that window are accommodated on a
best-effort basis and are not guaranteed.
3. Meal Service by Fare Class
Meal service depends on the fare class and flight duration:
  · Basic Economy and Economy: Complimentary meal on flights over 3 hours. Snacks and
    non-alcoholic beverages on shorter flights. Food available for purchase on
    flights without complimentary meal service.
  · Premium Economy: Complimentary meal on flights over 3 hours, with an enhanced
    menu and the ability to pre-select from the standard meal options.
  · Business Class: Premium multi-course meal service with menu selection on most
    flights.
  · First Class: Chef-curated multi-course meal service with premium menu selection.
All fare classes may request any of the standard meal options in Section 1 for
flights where a meal is served.
4. Allergies and Dietary Restrictions
4.1 Food Allergies
  · Inform the cabin crew of any food allergies before meal service.
  · Special meals can be pre-ordered for most allergies, such as gluten-free or
    dairy-free. See the Special Assistance Policy for allergy handling details.
  · We cannot guarantee that any meal is completely free of specific allergens due to
    shared preparation facilities.
4.2 Nut Allergies
We do not serve peanuts on any flight. Tree nut products may be served, and we cannot
guarantee a nut-free environment. For severe nut allergies, see the Special
Assistance Policy for buffer-zone arrangements.
5. Beverages
  · Non-alcoholic beverages (water, soft drinks, juice, coffee, tea) are
    complimentary on all flights.
  · Alcoholic beverages are complimentary in Premium Economy, Business, and First
    Class. In Economy and Basic Economy, alcoholic beverages are available for
    purchase on flights where service is offered.
6. Children's Meals
A child meal can be requested for passengers aged 2 to 11. Infant meals are available
on request. Child and infant meals follow the same 24-hour pre-order deadline as
other special meals.
7. Refunds for Meal Pre-Orders
If your flight is cancelled or significantly changed, any paid meal pre-orders are
refunded. See the Cancellation and Refund Policy for details.
