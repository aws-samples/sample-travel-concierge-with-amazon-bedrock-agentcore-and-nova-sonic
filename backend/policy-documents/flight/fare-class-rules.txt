Fare Class Rules
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
1. Fare Class Overview
Basic Economy: Seat assigned at check-in. Personal item only. Checked bags $35 first
bag domestic. No changes allowed after 24-hour window. No refund or credit on
cancellation. Last boarding group. Miles earned at 50%. Not upgrade eligible.
Economy: Free standard seat selection. Personal item plus carry-on. Checked bags $35
first bag domestic. Free changes (fare difference applies). Travel credit on
cancellation. General boarding. Miles earned at 100%. Upgrade eligible.
Premium Economy: Free preferred seat selection. Personal item plus carry-on. 1 free
checked bag all routes. Free changes (fare difference applies). Travel credit on
cancellation. Priority boarding. Complimentary meal on flights over 3 hours.
Complimentary WiFi. Miles earned at 125%. Upgrade eligible to Business.
Business Class: Free seat selection (all seats). Personal item plus carry-on. 2 free
checked bags up to 70 lbs each. Free changes (fare difference applies). Refundable
fare option available. Priority boarding, check-in, security, and baggage. Lie-flat
seat on long-haul international. Premium meal service. Complimentary WiFi. Lounge
access. Miles earned at 150%. Upgrade eligible to First.
First Class: Free seat selection (all seats). Personal item plus carry-on. 3 free
checked bags up to 70 lbs each. Free changes (fare difference applies). All fares
fully refundable. First boarding. Suite-style seating on select international routes.
Chef-curated multi-course meals. Complimentary WiFi, amenity kit, pajamas
(international). Lounge access including First Class lounge. Chauffeur service on
select routes. Miles earned at 200%.
2. Basic Economy Details
  · Seat assigned at check-in (no advance selection).
  · Boarding in the last group.
  · Overhead bin space not guaranteed; bag may be gate-checked at no charge.
  · No changes, cancellations, or upgrades after the 24-hour booking window.
  · Miles earned at 50% of the standard rate.
  · Not eligible for complimentary upgrades.
3. Economy Details
  · Free standard seat selection at booking.
  · Preferred seats (extra legroom, front of cabin) available for $15-$75 depending
on route.
  · Exit row seats available for $25-$95.
  · Changes allowed with no fee (fare difference applies).
  · Travel credit issued on cancellation.
  · Eligible for paid and mileage upgrades.
  · Eligible for complimentary upgrades (Gold tier and above).
4. Premium Economy Details
  · Wider seat with extra legroom (up to 38 inches pitch).
  · Preferred seat selection included at booking.
  · 1 free checked bag on all routes.
  · Complimentary meal service on flights over 3 hours.
  · Complimentary WiFi.
  · Priority boarding and check-in.
  · Miles earned at 125% of standard rate.
  · Eligible for paid and mileage upgrades to Business.
5. Business Class Details
  · Lie-flat seat on long-haul international flights.
  · Premium recliner seat on domestic and short-haul flights.
  · 2 free checked bags (up to 70 lbs each).
  · Premium meal service with menu selection.
  · Complimentary WiFi and noise-canceling headphones.
  · Lounge access at departure airport.
  · Priority boarding, check-in, security, and baggage handling.
  · Miles earned at 150% of standard rate.
  · Eligible for mileage upgrades to First Class.
  · Refundable fare option available.
6. First Class Details
  · Largest seat with maximum privacy and recline.
  · Suite-style seating on select international routes.
  · 3 free checked bags (up to 70 lbs each).
  · Chef-curated multi-course meal service.
  · Premium beverage selection including champagne.
  · Complimentary WiFi, amenity kit, and pajamas (international).
  · Lounge access including First Class lounge where available.
  · Chauffeur service to/from airport on select routes.
  · Priority everything: boarding, check-in, security, baggage.
  · Miles earned at 200% of standard rate.
  · All fares fully refundable.
7. Fare Class Codes
  · Basic Economy: B
  · Economy: Y, M, H, Q, K, L, V, W
  · Premium Economy: O, A
  · Business: J, C, D, I
  · First Class: F, P
