Cancellation and Refund Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
1. 24-Hour Flexible Booking Policy
You may cancel any ticket for a full refund to your original form of payment if you
cancel within 24 hours of booking, provided the flight is at least 7 days away. This
applies to all fare classes, including Basic Economy and non-refundable tickets. No
cancellation fee applies during this window.
2. Cancellation Rules by Fare Class
2.1 Basic Economy
  · Before departure: No cancellation allowed after the 24-hour window. Ticket value
is forfeited.
  · No-show: Full ticket value forfeited. No travel credit issued.
  · Exceptions: If the airline cancels or significantly changes the flight, a full
refund is provided.
2.2 Economy (Non-Refundable)
  · Before departure: Cancellation allowed. No cash refund. A travel credit is issued
for the ticket value minus any applicable cancellation fee.
  · Cancellation fee: $0 for domestic flights; $0 for international flights
(cancellation fees eliminated for standard Economy and above).
  · Travel credit validity: 12 months from the original ticket issue date.
  · No-show: Travel credit issued minus a $99 no-show fee.
2.3 Premium Economy
  · Before departure: Cancellation allowed. Travel credit issued for full ticket
value (no cancellation fee).
  · Travel credit validity: 12 months from original issue date.
  · No-show: Travel credit issued minus a $99 no-show fee.
2.4 Business Class
  · Refundable fares: Full refund to original form of payment at any time before
departure.
  · Non-refundable fares: Travel credit for full ticket value.
  · No-show: Refundable fares receive full refund. Non-refundable fares receive
travel credit minus $99.
2.5 First Class
  · All fares are refundable: Full refund to original form of payment at any time
before departure.
  · No-show: Full refund issued.
3. Refund Methods
3.1 Cash/Credit Card Refunds
  · Processed within 7 business days for credit card purchases.
  · Processed within 20 business days for cash/check purchases.
  · Refunded to the original form of payment.
3.2 Travel Credits
  · Issued as an electronic voucher tied to the original passenger name.
  · Valid for 12 months from the original ticket issue date.
  · Can be used for any future booking (fare difference applies if new ticket costs
more).
  · Non-transferable to other passengers.
  · Cannot be converted to cash.
4. Airline-Initiated Cancellations and Schedule Changes
4.1 Your Rights When We Cancel Your Flight
If we cancel your flight for any reason, you are entitled to:
  · A full refund to your original form of payment (regardless of fare class), OR
  · Rebooking on the next available flight at no additional cost, OR
  · Rebooking on a later flight of your choice (subject to availability).
4.2 Significant Schedule Changes
If we change your flight schedule by 3 or more hours (domestic) or 4 or more hours
(international), you are entitled to:
  · A full refund to your original form of payment, OR
  · Rebooking on an alternative flight at no additional cost.
4.3 Delay Compensation
If your flight is delayed by 90 minutes or more within 72 hours of departure:
  · You may be eligible for a full refund if you choose not to travel.
  · Meal vouchers provided for delays over 2 hours.
  · Hotel accommodation provided for overnight delays (airline-caused only, not
weather).
5. Involuntary Denied Boarding (Oversold Flights)
If you are involuntarily denied boarding due to an oversold flight:
  · We will first seek volunteers willing to give up their seats in exchange for
compensation.
  · If you are involuntarily bumped, you are entitled to cash compensation.
  · Domestic flights: Up to $775 if rebooked within 1-2 hours; up to $1,550 if
delayed more than 2 hours.
  · International flights: Up to $775 if rebooked within 1-4 hours; up to $1,550 if
delayed more than 4 hours.
  · You will also be rebooked on the next available flight at no cost.
6. Refund Eligibility for Additional Purchases
If your flight is cancelled or significantly changed, you are also entitled to
refunds for:
  · Checked baggage fees
  · Seat selection fees
  · WiFi packages purchased for the cancelled flight
  · Meal pre-orders
  · Any other ancillary services purchased for the affected flight
7. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund
  · Online: Log in to your account and select Manage Trip to cancel and request a
refund.
  · Phone: Call our customer service line. Available 24/7.
  · Airport: Visit any ticket counter for assistance.
  · Refund status: Check refund status online under Refund Requests in your account.
